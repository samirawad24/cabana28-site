Our Story

What Cabana 28 Stands For

Cabana 28 was born from a simple belief: great Peruvian food should taste like home. Every day we marinate our chicken in a traditional blend of Peruvian spices and roast it slowly until it's golden, juicy and full of smoky flavor — the way pollo a la brasa is meant to be.

We're a family-friendly neighborhood spot on N Andrews Ave where quality ingredients, generous portions and genuine hospitality come first. Whether you're joining us for dinner or ordering in, you'll taste the care in every plate.

  • Authentic Peruvian recipes & spices
  • Fresh ingredients, prepared daily
  • Warm, family-friendly dining room
  • Family meals made for sharing

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you visit or order.

Where is Cabana 28 located in Fort Lauderdale?

You'll find us at 6351 N Andrews Ave, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309 — just off N Andrews Ave with easy parking. Call us anytime at (954) 530-5123.

Does Cabana 28 offer delivery in Fort Lauderdale?

Yes! We deliver throughout the Fort Lauderdale area via DoorDash and Uber Eats. You can also order pickup directly through our online ordering site to skip delivery fees.

What is Cabana 28 known for?

Our Peruvian-style pollo a la brasa (slow-roasted rotisserie chicken) is the star, alongside lomo saltado, fresh ceviche, arroz chaufa and generous family meals made for sharing.

Is Cabana 28 family-friendly?

Absolutely. We're a warm, casual neighborhood restaurant where families are always welcome — with big portions and family combos that make dinner easy.

What are the best Peruvian dishes to try in Fort Lauderdale?

Start with pollo a la brasa and our green ají sauce, then work your way through lomo saltado, classic ceviche and arroz chaufa — Peru's famous fried rice. You can't go wrong.

Preguntas

Good to know

Where is Cabaña 28?

6351 N Andrews Ave, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309. There is parking in front of the restaurant.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 10:30am to 9pm, Saturday 10:15am to 9pm, Sunday 10:15am to 8pm.

Can I order pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order pickup on our online ordering site or by phone at (954) 530-5123. We deliver through DoorDash and Uber Eats.

What should I order the first time?

Start with pollo a la brasa and our green ají, then add lomo saltado or a ceviche for the table. Chaufa de pollo a la brasa is a good pick if you are eating alone.

Do you have meals for families and groups?

Yes. Our family meals start at $26.99 for a whole rotisserie chicken with two sides. For catering, call us or use the catering option on our ordering site.

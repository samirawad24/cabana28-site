Nuestra mesa

A family kitchen on North Andrews

Cabaña 28 is run by our family. The recipes are the ones we cook at home: chicken marinated the night before, huancaína made in the kitchen, and ají verde on every table.

Come in for a quarter chicken at lunch or bring everyone for a family meal. Either way, we want you to leave full.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cabaña 28 website collects and how it is used. It applies to this website only.

What we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or newsletter, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or payment details through it. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you call us, we only use what you tell us to answer your question or take your order.

Online ordering and delivery

When you click to order, you leave this website. Pickup orders are handled by orders.co, and delivery is handled by DoorDash and Uber Eats. Those companies collect the information needed to place your order, including payment details, under their own privacy policies. Please read their policies before you order.

Third-party services on this site

This website loads an embedded Google Map and fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser loads them, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Google's use of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs for security.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies or use advertising or tracking tools. The embedded Google Map may set cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the rest of the site will still work.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
